Status: Critical Warning Issued

SECTION 1: TIDAL FLAP GATE SEIZURE
- Visual inspection during lowest astronomical tide revealed Flap Gate #2 is mechanically jammed at an 18-degree open angle.
- Cause of seizure: Dense marine bio-fouling, oyster encrustation, and silt wedging in the phosphor-bronze trunnion hinge pin assembly.
- Seizure prevents automated back-sealing against incoming high tide, creating imminent danger of saline seawater back-inundation into low-lying transit corridors during spring tides exceeding 4.6m chart datum.

SECTION 2: INTAKE SILTATION AND ACTUATOR TELEMETRY
- Silt sedimentation depth in the approach channel measured at 1.85m, covering over 65% of the intake throat.
- Electro-hydraulic hoist cylinder #1 shows hydraulic oil leakage at the rod seal (0.5 liters/hour loss rate).
- Emergency diesel generator backup failed automatic transfer switch test during simulated power interruption.

RECOMMENDED INTERVENTIONS:
1. High-pressure steam cleaning and diver descaling of trunnion hinge pins within 14 days.
2. Hydraulic dredging of 120 meters of intake throat channel.
3. Cylinder seal overhaul and hydraulic fluid replacement.
