SECTION 1: PUMP MOTOR VIBRATION SPECTRUM ANALYSIS
Tri-axial accelerometer readings taken at bearing housing of High-Lift Pump Motor #3:
- Radial Vibration RMS Velocity: 7.82 mm/s (Peak Alert Threshold: 4.5 mm/s as per ISO 10816-3).
- Spectral Peak detected at 2x running speed (59.6 Hz), confirming impeller vane pass defect and angular misalignment with discharge manifold.
- High cavitation acoustic emission detected during raw intake turbidity fluctuations exceeding 180 NTU.

SECTION 2: CLARIFIER UNIT 3 SLUDGE SCRAPER MECHANISM
- Drive torque spikes observed up to 142% of maximum rated motor current during bottom sludge scraper sweeps.
- Severe mechanical friction detected in central turn-table bearing due to grit contamination and failing lip seal.
- Manual grab sample turbidity verified at 14.2 NTU whereas SCADA optical sensor reported 4.1 NTU, indicating a 10.1 NTU discrepancy due to sensor bio-fouling.

RECOMMENDED INTERVENTIONS:
1. Immediate isolation and overhaul of Pump #3 impeller and dynamic rotor balancing.
2. High-pressure water jetting of rapid gravity sand filter underdrain nozzles.
3. Recalibration and ultrasonic cleaning of intake turbidity sensor probes.
