SECTION 1: BARREL SILTATION AND INLET BLOCKAGE
- Physical sounding indicates 68% cross-sectional hydraulic blockage in the 2.5m x 2.0m RCC box barrel caused by accumulated granite scree, mudstone gravel, and uprooted vegetative debris.
- Effective discharge capacity reduced from design 18.5 m3/sec to 5.2 m3/sec, posing catastrophic road overtopping risk during monsoon precipitation events exceeding 65 mm/hr.
- Upstream inlet catch-pit filled with compacted sediment up to invert level.

SECTION 2: STRUCTURAL INTEGRITY AND SLOPE MOVEMENT
- Upstream left-bank masonry wingwall exhibits a diagonal shear fissure of 8mm width, caused by lateral earth pressure from saturated slope overburden.
- Inclinometer sensor stationed 15m uphill reports creep displacement of 12mm over 60 days.
- Invert concrete slab shows surface cavitation wear of 25mm depth along the central flow line.

RECOMMENDED INTERVENTIONS:
1. Urgent mechanical desilting and debris clearing of the culvert barrel before June 1st.
2. Installation of wire-mesh gabion retaining crates uphill to trap sliding scree.
3. Epoxy-grout injection into wingwall diagonal shear fissure.
